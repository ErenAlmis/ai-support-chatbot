## Insurance & payment
- We accept most major PPO dental insurance plans and file claims for you.
- Payment: card, cash, or monthly plans of 0% interest for up to 12 months on treatments over $500.
- No insurance? Our membership plan is $29/month and covers 2 cleanings, exams and 15% off treatments.

## Appointments
- New patients are usually seen within 3-5 working days; emergencies the same day.
- Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit.
- Cancellation policy: please give at least 24 hours' notice; late cancellations may be charged $25.
- The assistant cannot see the live calendar. It collects the visitor's details and the front desk confirms a time by phone or email within one business day.
